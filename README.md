# Screener X-Ray

A local, privacy-focused Chrome extension (Manifest V3) for [screener.in](https://www.screener.in) company pages.

Screener X-Ray reorganizes company financial data into a clean, descriptive equity-research one-pager, sets out financial statements in statutory Indian formats (Schedule III / Banking Regulation Act / Ind AS 7), provides 10 context-aware charts, and enables private investment thesis notes.

## Key Features

- **Strictly Local**: Zero telemetry, zero external network calls, zero analytics. Everything runs on your machine.
- **Descriptive Only**: Contains no scores, grades, ratings, price targets, or buy/sell/hold calls.
- **Indian Filing Formats**: Schedule III Division II (Companies), Banking Regulation Act Third Schedule (Banks), Division III (NBFCs), and Ind AS 7 (Cash Flow).
- **Adaptive Charts**: 10 sector-tailored SVG charts without external charting libraries.
- **Private Notes**: Saves your notes locally per company on your device.
